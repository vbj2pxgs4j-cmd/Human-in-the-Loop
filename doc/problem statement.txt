Problem statement
Support and product teams at an Indian investment platform are each looking at half of the same problem. Support answers the same fee and charge questions dozens of times a month. Each reply is written fresh and sometimes comes out wrong. Product reads a review dashboard that says "pricing confusion is up" but not which fee is confusing, or what support is already telling users about it.
The information that would connect the two teams is in no single place: what the official documents actually say, what users are actually confused about, and how often. As a result, the same fee is explained inconsistently, and product decisions are made without knowing which questions keep coming back.
What we are building
One deployed, human-approved web application that runs both halves on a schedule and links them.
Weekly loop: Ingest reviews, cluster them into at most five themes, and identify the top three along with the recurring fee or charge confusion. Produce a pulse of 250 words or fewer with supporting quotes and three action ideas. Also produce a fee explainer of six bullets or fewer, with two official source links and a last-checked date.
Response loop: Take a queue of incoming user questions. Each one runs through three agents in parallel, then an orchestrator, then a reviewer. The result is a cited reply draft of three sentences or fewer, plus an internal routing note.
Connection: An approved fee explainer becomes the canonical answer the response loop reuses instead of drafting fresh. Any question seen five or more times in a week appears in the weekly pulse.
Constraints, enforced in code
Facts only: no recommendations, no buy or sell language, and no return or performance figures.
Every factual claim carries one source link from the Milestone 1 corpus, and only public sources are used.
When the corpus doesn't cover a question, the system says so and names what it searched.
No PII is stored anywhere, including the dataset, interface, logs and agent state.
Nothing is written or sent without a human approving that specific action.
Success criteria
Every question is answered or explained. Every queued question produces either a draft of three sentences or fewer, or an explicit "not covered" response that names what it searched.
The weekly loop runs unattended and inside its caps. Once triggered, the loop completes end-to-end without manual intervention. The pulse stays within 250 words and the explainer within six bullets. Every theme traces to review ids and a stated window.
Edits and approvals stay contained. Editing, approving or rejecting one item changes only that item. A diff of system state before and after each action confirms it.
Claims are grounded and nothing sensitive leaks. In a sample of fifteen claims across both loops, each traces to an official source link or a review id. Advice requests, return-figure requests, and messages containing a PAN, account number or phone number are refused or redacted every time, rephrased and repeated, in both loops. Nothing sensitive reaches logs or agent state.
